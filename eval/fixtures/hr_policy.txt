Employee Leave Policy

All full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per year, credited
at the start of each calendar year. Unused vacation days may be carried
over into the following year, up to a maximum of 5 days.

Sick leave is separate from vacation and is capped at 10 days per year.
Employees must notify their manager before 9am on any day of unplanned
sick leave.

Parental leave is granted at 12 weeks paid for the primary caregiver and
6 weeks paid for the secondary caregiver, in line with company policy
updated in 2025.
