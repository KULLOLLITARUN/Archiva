Product Roadmap — Q3 2026

The Q3 2026 roadmap centers on two initiatives: launching the mobile app
on iOS and Android, and expanding availability to the European market.

The mobile app launch is scheduled for August 2026 and will ship with
offline mode and push notifications in the first release. European
expansion begins with Germany and France, targeted for September 2026,
pending completion of GDPR compliance review.

Longer term, the team is exploring a public API for third-party
integrations, tentatively planned for Q1 2027.
