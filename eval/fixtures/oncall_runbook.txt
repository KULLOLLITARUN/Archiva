Engineering On-Call Runbook

Oncall engineers must acknowledge a paging alert within 15 minutes of it
firing. If an alert is not acknowledged within 15 minutes, it automatically
escalates to the secondary oncall engineer, and after 30 minutes to the
engineering manager.

Severity 1 incidents (full outage) require a status page update within
10 minutes of acknowledgment. Severity 2 incidents (partial degradation)
require a status page update within 30 minutes.

All incidents must have a postmortem written within 5 business days of
resolution.
